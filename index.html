ACO Mathematical Working Principle

State Transition Rule (Probabilistic Choice of Ant \(k\) from node \(i\) to \(j\)):

$$P_{ij}^k = \frac{(\tau_{ij})^\alpha \cdot (\eta_{ij})^\beta}{\sum_{l \in \text{allowed}_k} (\tau_{il})^\alpha \cdot (\eta_{il})^\beta}$$
1. Pheromone Concentration (\(\tau_{ij}\)) Represents collective swarm memory. When an ant completes a tour, it deposits pheromone inversely proportional to path length \(\Delta \tau = Q / L_k\).
2. Heuristic Desirability (\(\eta_{ij}\)) Local visibility heuristic, calculated as \(\eta_{ij} = \frac{1}{d_{ij}}\) where \(d_{ij}\) is Euclidean distance or delay cost between nodes.
3. Evaporation Decay (\(\rho\)) Updated via \(\tau_{ij} \leftarrow (1-\rho)\tau_{ij}\). Evaporation eliminates poor decisions and prevents global stagnation in local optima.
4. Stigmergic Communication Indirect coordination through environmental modification. Ants do not talk directly; they communicate via chemical density trails.
